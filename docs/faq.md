## Product & History

### What is InstaReport?

InstaReport is a premium social media automation platform developed and maintained by **iExly** — combining an automated reporting engine, account management workflows, a cloud-hosted Telegram bot, profile lookup utilities and a secure online license system.

### What is the relationship between Credly, iEscly and iExly?

- **2021:** Started as **Credly**.
- **2022 – 2026:** Known as **iEscly**.
- **2026 – Present:** Published under the **iExly** organization as **InstaReport**.

### Is InstaReport open source?

No. InstaReport is **commercial proprietary software**. The codebase is distributed exclusively through official, license-protected channels. See the [License Guide](../LICENSE_GUIDE.md).

## Features

### Which platforms can I report on?

The engine supports **11 platforms**: Instagram, YouTube, X (Twitter), Telegram, Discord, Reddit, TikTok, Facebook, Snapchat, Threads and Gmail.

### What report reasons are available?

8 reasons: **Spam, Harassment, Impersonation, Hate speech, Nudity, Violence, Misinformation, Scam/Fraud** — with localized wording for multiple languages.

### Can I schedule automatic reports?

Yes — the Telegram bot's `/schedule` command repeats the last report configuration at a set time each day, with optional repetition and interval.

### Can I bulk import targets?

Yes — the batch processor includes a **📂 Import .txt** button (one target per line, `#` for comments).

### Does it handle 2FA / OTP?

Yes — OTP collection is handled via Telegram with a 120-second timeout window.

### Does it use proxies?

Yes — configurable proxies with automatic blacklisting after repeated failures.

## Licensing

### How does activation work?

Purchase → launch → enter key → validated online and bound to your device hardware ID.

### Can I share my license?

No. Licenses are hardware-bound to a single device.

### What happens if I lose my device?

Contact support via the official Telegram channel with your purchase details.

### Which operating systems are supported?

### Is InstaReport safe to use?
